# 📝 Notes

## Audio Philosophy

- Gameplay timing is more important than perfect sounds.
- Replace placeholder sounds only after mechanics feel right.
- Favor short, satisfying sounds over realistic ones.
- Use randomized pitch and multiple variations to prevent repetition.

## Design Philosophy

- Easy to learn.
- Difficult to master.
- Quick restarts.
- Every hole teaches something.
- Reward creativity.
- Prioritize satisfying game feel over realism.
- Polish should enhance gameplay, never distract from it.
